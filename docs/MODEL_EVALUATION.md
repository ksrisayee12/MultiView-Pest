## 3. Error Analysis and Observations

1. **Spider Mite Bias on High-Frequency Lesions:**
   The fine-tuned checkpoint (`NarimanOsman2025/tomato-disease-detector-yolov8n`) shows a high recall bias towards two-spotted spider mites on leaves featuring speckling or chlorosis (`sample_early_blight_001` and `sample_healthy_001`).
2. **Septoria Leaf Spot Robustness:**
   Small circular punctate lesions with gray centers (`sample_septoria_001`) were correctly identified with high confidence (0.70).
3. **Domain Gap & Sample Size Limitations:**
   With N=4 held-out samples, the 95% Wilson confidence interval for accuracy is `[0.0456, 0.6994]`. A larger held-out benchmark across thousands of in-the-wild images from PlantDoc is recommended for production field deployment.
